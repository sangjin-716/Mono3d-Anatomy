STATISTICS WE DECLINE TO COMPUTE AT THIS n, AND WHY
  * CLUSTER BOOTSTRAP CI on the slope. NOT COMPUTED. Resampling G=3 clusters with
    replacement puts 3/27 = 11.1% of draws on a single repeated cluster, and any
    draw that omits both non-singleton families cannot identify a slope at all.
    The cluster bootstrap has no valid coverage at G=3. CR0 with t(G-1) is reported
    in its place -- and CR0 is itself known to be anticonservative at G=3.
  * FAMILY-MEAN REGRESSION. NOT FITTED. Collapsing to family means leaves n=3
    points, df=1, one of them a single detector. The three means are printed above
    as description only; no line is fitted to them and no p-value is reported.
  * A p-VALUE FROM THE CR0 FIT. NOT REPORTED. With df = G-1 = 2 (or 1) the t
    critical value is 4.303 (or 12.706) and any resulting 'p' would be theatre.
    We report the interval and the MDES instead.

6. IS THE RESIDUAL SCATTER JUST EVALUATION NOISE?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Only frozen variance figure available without re-running a bootstrap:
  reports/final_run/e3_endpoint_gap_boot.txt L8
  'M3D-RPN->MonoIA  point dGap=+0.046 CI[-1.888,+1.816]' (paired drive resample, B=1000)
  CI half-width                        = 1.852 AP
  implied SD of the paired contrast    = 0.945 AP  (half-width / 1.96)
  implied per-detector eval-noise SD  <= 0.668 AP  (contrast SD / sqrt 2)

  [HEADLINE-TREND  CEIL* (M/n_gt)      [gate -0.476]]
     OLS residual SD = 0.683 AP  ->  residual / noise-floor = 1.02 x
  [_NATIVE true-IoU, SHIPPED cells     [gate -0.629]]
     OLS residual SD = 1.225 AP  ->  residual / noise-floor = 1.83 x
  [_NATIVE true-IoU, make_figs.py as-is [2/12 STALE]]
     OLS residual SD = 1.336 AP  ->  residual / noise-floor = 2.00 x

READ, stated per basis rather than as one claim:
  * On the CEIL* basis the residual SD (0.683 AP) is at the drive-resampling noise
    floor (0.668 AP), ratio 1.02. On that basis there is no resolvable EXCESS
    between-detector heterogeneity: the only lever on resolution is more detectors.
  * On the true-IoU basis the residual SD is 1.225 AP, 1.83 x the noise floor. That
    basis carries real extra scatter, and section 5 identifies its largest source:
    the per-detector true-IoU understatement (MonoDGP +3.18, MonoCLUE +1.66,
    MonoCon +0.87 AP), which the CEIL* basis removes by construction.
  * We therefore do NOT claim the panel scatter is 'all noise'. We claim it on the
    CEIL* basis only, which is the basis the paper headlines.
CAVEATS: the per-detector figure is an UPPER bound inferred from ONE frozen paired
  CI, not from a per-detector bootstrap; the paired common-drive resample removes
  shared drive variance (making the bound conservative) and the sqrt(2) step assumes
  the two arms' residual noise is independent. NO bootstrap was re-run for this
  script. A per-detector drive-clustered bootstrap of the gap would be the correct
  upgrade and was NOT performed here.
