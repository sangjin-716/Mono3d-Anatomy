dgp_cop_dump.py keeps every query, with its maximum class probability as `cls`, and decodes
it with the Car mean size. Part B shows that the MonoDGP and MonoCoP checkpoints are Car-only.
MonoDLE: the adapter writes the K=50 heatmap top-k with no score threshold and drops non-Car
peaks, so 50 rows in every image means no Pedestrian or Cyclist peak in any val top-50.
RESULT A: every released dump holds Car candidates only, including the full-validation
dumps of GUPNet (gupnet_val.csv) and MonoCon (monocon_val.csv).
