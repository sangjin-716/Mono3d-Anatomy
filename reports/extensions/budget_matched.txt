HONEST RESIDUAL
  Inside a matched cell, pool size and recall* are still positively rank-associated
  At N=5 the budget spread is only 1.09x, so this
  cannot be a budget effect; it reflects that stronger detectors emit more distinct
  post-NMS boxes AND localise better. It is an association, not a cause, either way.
  Budgets can only be matched DOWNWARD: query models are architecturally capped at 50
  queries and five CenterNet dumps hold >=50 Car candidates on 0.000 of images
  (reports/extensions/budget_saturation.txt), so N=20 is the largest common budget.
